# SynapseNet

A dynamic, AI-curated skill graph backed by blockchain-verified credentials and contributions, where users don’t just list skills—they prove them through traceable, trustless interactions.


## What It Does:
- AI analyzes real-world contributions (code commits, designs, papers, forum answers, etc.) and maps them to a personalized skill graph.
- Blockchain verifies provenance of each skill artifact—who did what, when, and how it evolved.
- Smart endorsements: Instead of likes or vague recommendations, peers can stake tokens to endorse specific skills, which are weighted by their own verified expertise.
- Skill NFTs: Users can mint non-transferable NFTs representing mastery in a domain, tied to on-chain evidence and AI validation.

## Why It’s Game-Changing:
- Trustless credibility: No need to rely on self-declared resumes or unverifiable endorsements.
- AI-powered growth: Users get personalized skill-building paths based on gaps in their graph.
- Decentralized meritocracy: Reputation is earned, not claimed—and it’s portable across ecosystems.
- Tokenized incentives: Contributors to open-source, education, or mentorship can earn tokens tied to verified impact.
