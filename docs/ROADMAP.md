# Delivery roadmap

| Phase | Status | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | Complete | Product foundation, credential workflows, trust authority, and primary clients. |
| 4 | Engineering complete | Governed multi-organization rollout and production readiness. |
| 5 | Engineering complete | Continuous monitoring, alerting, and incident readiness. |
| 6 | Engineering complete | Capacity, resilience, and tamper-evident release evidence. |
| 7 | Engineering complete | Code, dependency, secret, vulnerability, and SBOM assurance. |
| 8 | Engineering complete | Disaster recovery objectives, evidence, and recovery sequence. |
| 9 | Engineering complete | Independent approval gate and commit-bound go-live certificate. |

No further repository implementation phase is currently defined. Production launch remains an
execution activity: onboard real legal organizations, provision external infrastructure and
secrets, run the documented drills, resolve security findings, collect approvals, and issue the
Phase 9 certificate. Those actions require real operators and cannot be truthfully completed with
example manifests or generated approval records.
